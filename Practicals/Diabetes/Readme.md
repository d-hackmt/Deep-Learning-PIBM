# Diabetes (PIMA) Dataset

This project contains the **PIMA Indians Diabetes Dataset**, which is often used for learning and testing machine learning models. It is a **classification dataset**, meaning we try to predict if a person has diabetes or not.

## About the Dataset

The dataset was created by the National Institute of Diabetes and Digestive and Kidney Diseases.  
All the people in the dataset are **women** who are **21 years or older** and are **Pima Indian** by heritage.  
The goal is to use the medical information in the dataset to predict if a person has diabetes.

## What is Diabetes?

**Diabetes** is a health condition where the amount of sugar (glucose) in the blood is too high for a long time.  
This happens when:
- The body does not make enough insulin (a hormone that controls blood sugar), or
- The body cannot use insulin properly.

If diabetes is not treated, it can cause serious health problems like:
- Heart disease
- Kidney problems
- Nerve damage
- Eye problems or blindness

### Types of Diabetes:
- **Type 1:** The body attacks the cells that make insulin (autoimmune disease).
- **Type 2:** The body stops using insulin well (often linked with being overweight or not active).
- **Gestational:** Happens during pregnancy because of hormone changes.

## Columns in the Dataset

| Column Name                  | What it means |
|------------------------------|---------------|
| `Pregnancies`                | How many times the person has been pregnant |
| `Glucose`                    | Blood sugar level after a test |
| `BloodPressure`              | Blood pressure (lower number, mm Hg) |
| `SkinThickness`              | Skin fold thickness on the back of the arm (mm) |
| `Insulin`                    | Amount of insulin in the blood after 2 hours |
| `BMI`                        | Body Mass Index = weight(kg) / height(m)^2 |
| `DiabetesPedigreeFunction`   | A score that shows the chance of diabetes based on family history |
| `Age`                        | Age in years |
| `Outcome`                    | 0 = No diabetes, 1 = Diabetes |
